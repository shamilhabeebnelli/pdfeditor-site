Honest scope. Whiteout hides content visually; in a normal export the covered data can still exist inside the file. For irreversible masking, tick Flatten export (the page becomes an image and its text is no longer selectable; flatten draws every script your device can display, as image). Text edits keep the original position, size and alignment and use the closest matching built-in font (bold/italic adjustable with the B/I buttons); if the document's own embedded font is not a standard family, the replacement can look slightly different - the editor marks those lines when you click them. A replacement wider than the original asks before covering whatever stands to its right. Direct text edits support Latin-script text; for other scripts use Flatten export (text becomes part of the page image, not selectable). Scanned (image-only) pages have no editable text, but whiteout and added text still work.
